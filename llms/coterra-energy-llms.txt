# Coterra Energy

> Coterra Energy Inc. was a Houston-based independent oil and natural gas exploration and production company (Permian Basin, Marcellus Shale, Anadarko Basin), formed in October 2021 from Cabot Oil & Gas and Cimarex Energy. On May 7, 2026 it completed an all-stock merger with Devon Energy; the combined company operates as Devon Energy (NYSE: DVN). Coterra never operated a public developer program or API.
